How long are the proofs each checkpoint finds?

Every distinct Lean-checked proof from the 10,240-attempt textbook72 run, binned by body lines (lines after the premise block). Bars count proofs: pretrain found 403 in total, the 3 h checkpoint 14,117 and the 5.4 h checkpoint 27,410.

Pretrain stays inside its training cap. It was pretrained only on proofs of ≤ 6 body lines, and 99.5% of its proofs are ≤ 6 lines; the longest is 7. After RL, 80% (3 h) and 89% (5.4 h) of written proofs run past 6 lines.

Part of the length is padding. 81% (3 h) and 87% (5.4 h) of RL proofs contain lines the conclusion never depends on, against 31% for pretrain. Pruning those lines pulls the RL medians from 9 and 10 down to 7 and 8, and the longest proof from 41 lines to 23.

More RL mostly moves the tail. The two RL shapes peak at the same length. The 5.4 h checkpoint puts more mass beyond 14 written lines: p90 goes from 12 to 17.

How to read it. Proofs are pooled across problems, and the models solve different problems (15, 42 and 43 of 72), so part of the RL shift is harder problems being solved at all. Problems with many distinct proofs dominate: the busiest single problem contributes about 2,400 proofs to each RL histogram. Distinct means distinct within each 1,024-sample chunk, so a proof found in several of the 10 chunks counts once per chunk. Pruned keeps the last line and every line it reaches through citations (a needed box keeps its hypothesis and conclusion), as in harness.body_lines.
