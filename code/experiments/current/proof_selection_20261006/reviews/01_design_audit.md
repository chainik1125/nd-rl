Design audit of the supplied plans and source at nd-takehome `4dcd0e4f`; sprint branch `dmitry/selection-sprint`, HEAD `b3b8a583`. Checks below are recommendations unless explicitly reported as executed.

1. **HIGH — Lossy collection prevents exact scoring.** **Evidence:** [schema:19–25](/data/users/dmitry/ndrl-selection-sprint/code/experiments/current/proof_selection_20261006/schema.md:19) stores decoded actions without EOS; [lean_tok.py:184–193](/data/users/dmitry/runs/proof-selection/nd-takehome/lean_tok.py:184) also discards pad tokens. [state_sample.py:76–85](/data/users/dmitry/runs/proof-selection/nd-takehome/state_sample.py:76) distinguishes no-EOS truncation, application failure, and step cap. Thus decoded actions cannot recover every sampled prefix or termination. **Concrete check:** Before collection, retain raw IDs through actual EOS, per-action emitted lengths and EOS presence, plus decoded actions. Include failed/truncated actions. Never append EOS to a truncated action; exclude post-EOS padding but retain genuinely sampled pads. I executed a tokenizer probe confirming this information loss.

2. **HIGH — SN masking is underspecified.** **Evidence:** [worker B:25–27](/data/users/dmitry/ndrl-selection-sprint/code/experiments/current/proof_selection_20261006/briefs/worker_B_stopping.md:25) mentions the name after `have`; [state_env.py:357–374](/data/users/dmitry/runs/proof-selection/nd-takehome/state_env.py:357) also overwrites implication/negation and first-`Or.elim` lambda binders. These raw names were sampled, although their bindings are overridden. **Concrete check:** Mask those introduction positions from diagnostic likelihood/entropy aggregates; retain citation names, including `exact`, atomic references, and the `Or.elim` source. Keep raw names in conditioning prefixes. Exclude environment-inserted closures and second-branch binders ([419–445](/data/users/dmitry/runs/proof-selection/nd-takehome/state_env.py:419)). Report masked and full likelihood separately; masking is not an exact marginal probability over equivalent actions.

3. **HIGH — Replay equality of final ND is insufficient.** **Evidence:** [worker A:29–30](/data/users/dmitry/ndrl-selection-sprint/code/experiments/current/proof_selection_20261006/briefs/worker_A_selection.md:29) checks final text only. [state_sample.py:29–31](/data/users/dmitry/runs/proof-selection/nd-takehome/state_sample.py:29) encodes each current state; [lean_tok.py:125–126](/data/users/dmitry/runs/proof-selection/nd-takehome/lean_tok.py:125) makes SN history-free. [state_env.py:461–531](/data/users/dmitry/runs/proof-selection/nd-takehome/state_env.py:461) canonicalizes/decomposes at base zero. **Concrete check:** Compare collection-time and replayed prompt IDs before every action, using stored base and `assign=True`; never reconstruct likelihoods from ND alone. Score each action separately with shifted next-token targets, masking state/right-padding losses and retaining actual EOS. Compare batched teacher forcing with cached tokenwise logits, including failures and box transitions. T=1 is diagnostic, not the T=0.8 sampling likelihood.

4. **HIGH — “Completion” contains symbolic correctness information.** **Evidence:** [state_env.py:251–263](/data/users/dmitry/runs/proof-selection/nd-takehome/state_env.py:251) checks scope, last statement, and goal equality; [297–337](/data/users/dmitry/runs/proof-selection/nd-takehome/state_env.py:297) checks binder formulas and disjunction shape. Evaluate finished candidates separately. My environment probe reached `done=True` with an ill-typed conjunction projection from P: completion is not full verification.

8. **MED — Beating likelihood need not establish semantic recognition.** **Evidence:** [worker A:41–48](/data/users/dmitry/ndrl-selection-sprint/code/experiments/current/proof_selection_20261006/briefs/worker_A_selection.md:41) trains with labels, selects by pooled dev AUROC, and clips early pairs. **Hypotheses:** gains reflect supervised calibration, theorem difficulty, completion/length cues, memorization, or clipping artifacts. **Concrete check:** Add a supervised shallow baseline combining completion, likelihood, entropy, length, and base; measure within-theorem discrimination among finished candidates and clipping-stratified selection gains. A theorem-only score cannot rank a theorem’s candidates, but can inflate pooled AUROC and improve stopping allocation.

Define solvability conditional on the evaluated m-subset, retain unsolvable pools in headline accuracy, and flag zero oracle-minus-random denominators. Preselect primary k=8 comparisons; pointwise intervals do not establish dominance everywhere.
