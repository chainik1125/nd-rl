**Question (Dan):** `olmo-ifomega`
([summary](../../experiment-summaries/2026-11-04-olmo-ifomega-reach-on-ifbench-49-strict-43-hand-and-ifeval-6-omega-test-out-inconclusive/README.md))

- IFBench, 43 prompts by hand;
- IFEval, 6 prompts;
- OMEGA geometry, only where the DPO model is cut at the 32,768-token cap.

These are the memo's three cheapest decisive tests of the alternatives:

| test | what | memo estimate |
|---|---|---|
| 1. token budget | DPO on the three geometry reach prompts with ≈ 65k tokens (the model's full context), 64 samples each, both graders, hand-read | $8–12 |
| 2. brevity control | DPO with an "answer in at most 60 words" hint on the 3 letter-avoidance IFEval prompts and 10 IFBench reach prompts, 64 samples each; `s2300` at 16 as a check | ≈ $3 + hand checks |
| 3. deeper sampling | DPO to 1,024 samples on 10 IFBench hand-reach prompts | $30–40 |
| 4. optional | `step_0750` confirmation on the IFBench reach set | $5–7 |
