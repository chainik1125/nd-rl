# Research philosophy


## What is this document? - WIP
Welcome to our research group! This is a document meant to outline the research philosophy, or the research culture, of this project. 

## Notes

1. The most important goal of this research project is your own development.

    - If at any time you feel this project is not the best use of your time, please feel free to drop out. There is no judgement attached to dropping out. I will not feel like you have let me, or your other team members, down. On the contrary, I'm happy that you have something that is more impactful for you, and I would be upset if I am preventing you from doing that!

    - 

2. My goal is to be multiplicative, not additive.
    - My primary goal is to uplift your own work. 
        - I believe this research is important, and I will get excited about working on things at a ground level with you. 

3. Criticism is always valuable, but please bear in mind that this is the lowest form of utility.

    - TODO: Pyramid of value (Criticism < Constructive direction < Result < Leadership) (be a leader for our group!)

4. Hold ourselves to a higher standard of informativeness for the community

    - Compare to the strongest relevant baseline, not the weakest one you can get away with.

    - If this research is not useful, we should stop pursuing it and invest our, and the community's, time elsewhere.

5. Be excited about being scooped!
    - Try not to treat related work as a threat. There is a lot to do, and if someone solves one of the intermediate steps along the road - that's great.
    

6. Artifacts sustain momentum

    - Try to produce a tangible output from each week's or session's work. This makes your progress legible to the rest of the group and allows us to benefit from multiple minds thinking about it.
