2. **kalmar-complexity is reviewed: the program is short, and program length is the wrong measure of difficulty**
   ([summary](../../experiment-summaries/2026-10-20-kalmar-complexity-5443-bit-program-per-step-demand-not-program-length-ranks-kal-d-errors/README.md);
   [plain-language summary, with the review's corrections marked](../../experiment-summaries/2026-10-20-kalmar-complexity-5443-bit-program-per-step-demand-not-program-length-ranks-kal-d-errors/plain_summary.md)).
   You asked for the minimum description length of Kalmár's construction, as a handle on how many bits a model must
   absorb to learn it, and invited pushback. The learned numbers are kalmar-pretrain's KAL-D (9,560,832 params,
   `lean_staten`, from scratch on K12-Kalmár, 3 seeds) and new small models (0.4–9.6 M, from scratch). No proof is
   judged.
   - **The program is a few kilobits.** The shared-lemma algorithm alone is 5,443 bits of binary λ-calculus and 864 B
     of compressed Python, and 6,859 λ-calculus bits with parsing and rendering. It is the largest of nine calibration
     algorithms in every language: against truth-table validity, 13.3× in λ-calculus, 3.9× in Python, 7.8× in RASP
     operations. Every program reproduces the builder on the reviewer's fresh sample.
   - **Absorbed bits depend on how they are counted.** KAL-D's first epoch is 6.3–7.1 Mbit against its final model's
     held-out loss, 1.7–2.1 Mbit against its loss at the end of the epoch: 310–1,300× the program. The new block-wise
     runs give 1.1–1.5 Mbit, of which 52–73 % is the first block's uniform code.
   - **Your pushback question.** KAL-D's error by step type is ranked by per-step computational demand (an extended
     RASP program's effective depth ρ +0.85, in-scope lookups +0.82; 15 types), not by the length of the code that
     emits the step (−0.08 given frequency; within the 9 leaf types −0.80, or −0.68 given frequency). The run
     recommends, and the review accepts if the residual is named, stating "how much there is to learn" as a
     learner-relative excess description length under a stated protocol, plus a per-step demand profile.
   - **The causal test is confounded.** Showing the next needed subformula cuts opener-step error 94 % (3 / 3 paired
     seeds), but that subformula is the formula the step writes, so search and copying are not separated.

   cumulative RL / pretraining FLOPs, from an arm at ≈ 10⁻³ of Stage-1 (Yue's regime) to the ladders' ≈ 10

   capability?** style-edl saw no style cost for small differences (6–15 % of theorems differ; resolution about ±0.4

   whether the team's base-vs-RL EDL gaps (the EDL note) can be read as capability. The review's two next measurements
   test it: Kalmár with a capability-preserving fine-tune, and the shortest proofs on differing theorems only, with a
   stratified split and a fixed learning rate.

   subformula cuts KAL-D-like models' opener-step error 94 %, but the shown subformula is the formula the step

5. **Does K12 supply the reductio form, or does RL-time replay re-supply it?** The full arm replays 20,000 pretraining
   records per EI round, σ templates included. A full-arm ladder whose replay excludes σ separates the two.

   the as-run RL share.
