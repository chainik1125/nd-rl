## Numbers that cleared review

3.2 M models; acceptance always `nd_verify` ∧ Lean on the literal sampled text; 83,513 counted
proofs; sampler fixed at batch 2048, `max_new` 400, T 0.8 everywhere):

  transfer theorems: **C0 923 / 1003 (`L*` 12 / 11)**, R1 837 / 945, R3 898 / 698, R4 771 / 896,
  R2 658 / 800 (11 everywhere else, R2 s0 at 10). **The control is first of five on both seeds**

  mean and best frozen dial — has the worst ladder mean (729, ×0.757).** No arm satisfies proposal
  10's decision rule; the control set is not replaced.

  C0 0.9615, R2 0.9563, R4 0.9526, R3 0.9387). The whole visible arm difference is the **zero-shot
  depth-3 slice**, and that difference is **bookkeeping, not logic**: Spearman ρ = **−0.933**
  between an arm-seed's depth-3 rate and its `LEANPARSE` grammar-violation count, over 24 models.
- **The rendering effect itself is not established.** Depth-3 slice at n = 6: C0 0.533 (sd 0.256),
