\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:repro}

\paragraph{Code, data and models.} All code is in the fork \texttt{dan-pandori/nd-takehome}. The experiment is on branch
\texttt{dan\_create-or-elicit} (commit \texttt{8e195390}, with its review): scripts \texttt{coe/} and \texttt{pod/coe/},
the independent recount code \texttt{review\_coe/}, the pre-registration, per-round statistics, budgets, the estimator
calibration and a claims ledger. This paper is on \texttt{dan\_create-or-elicit-paper}. Its figures and statistics are
regenerated by \texttt{paper/scripts/} from those files, and every number in the text has a row in
\texttt{paper/LEDGER.md} that points to its source. The pretraining sets, all 200 ladder checkpoints, the evaluation rows,
the large-$k$ samples and the Lean-gate dumps are in the public bucket
\texttt{hf://buckets/dan-pandori/nd-rl/create-or-elicit/}. The decompressed md5 sums of the pretraining sets are \KT{}
\texttt{800b5486}, \KTko{} \texttt{466fc214} and \KTdose{} \texttt{19cd2263}.

\paragraph{Pre-registration and review.} The experiment's pre-registration was committed before its first GPU pod. Six
dated amendments each precede the work they govern, except the strict-set amendment (\S\ref{sec:primary}), which came
after round 1 of the first knockout ladder and before any acquisition outcome. The independent recount re-derived the experiment's
counts in \S\ref{sec:results} from the raw files; numbers marked \emph{this paper} are computed here from those files.
The experiment's pre-registration was written after the earlier full seeds' ladders had run, so their training-side
dynamics were known when it was written. The review corrected three Boschloo $p$-values that had been computed on a
transposed table; no verdict changed (Appendix~\ref{app:corrections}). This paper's own checks and analyses were
pre-registered as well (\texttt{preregistration/create-or-elicit-paper.md}, Appendix~\ref{sec:checks}). A2, A5
and Addendum 5 (A3b, A5b and descriptives such as the hardware-matched and guided gains comparisons) are post hoc, as are
the statistics marked \emph{this paper} that no pre-registration lists (among them the RL-phase Mann--Whitney test, the
pre-registered indicator applied at r0, the \dcov{} comparison and the certified counts by $N_{\mathrm{eval}}$). A3's two extensions and A4 were
registered as exploratory before they were computed. The experiment's pre-registered expectations are compared with outcomes in
Appendix~\ref{app:expectations}.

\paragraph{Compute.} Table~\ref{tab:compute} gives the compute per arm. Every new 16-round ladder made 2,301,440 attempts
on training theorems and 9,600 fine-tuning steps, and the knockout and dose ladders' compute is within 0.95--1.03 times
the full ladders' on every quantity. The experiment used 16 GPU instances (15 NVIDIA A40 at \$0.59/h and one RTX A6000)
for 309.34 instance-hours and \$182.50. This paper's checks used two A40 instances for 6.48 instance-hours and \$3.82.
