This writing run pre-registered its own checks (\texttt{preregistration/create-or-elicit-paper.md}, commit
\texttt{c602e43c} and Addenda 1--5) before running them. The pod checks used two NVIDIA A40 instances. All evaluations
are $k=256$, $T=0.8$, batch 512, on the 106 strict held-out theorems unless stated, with Lean in the loop.

\paragraph{C1: what the cut-offs hide.} Paired evaluations of ten r16 checkpoints (knockout s0--s5, dose s0--s2, and full s4,
the full seed with the most strict cut-offs) on the 106, with sample seed 0, standard caps (512 tokens per step, 96 steps) and doubled caps (1,024, 192). The pre-registration
assumed the paired draws would differ only where a cap binds. They do not: the sampler keys its noise by batch slot, so
an attempt that runs past a cap moves later attempts to other slots. After the first such
attempt the two evaluations are largely fresh draws, and a difference of a theorem or two can be sampling noise as well
as a lifted cut-off. For the same reason the standard-cap evaluation is itself a fresh draw: it reads a different input
file from the main evaluation, which is why knockout s1 shows 3 here and dose s2 shows 28, against 2 and 30 in
Table~\ref{tab:primary}. The knockout s5 pair ran on two A40 instances, from the same checkpoint.

The pre-registered expectation that doubled caps would cut the cut-off share at least five-fold missed on nine of the
ten checkpoints; it held only on knockout s0, at 0.04\%. Most cut attempts do not terminate, and they stay cut at twice
the budget. The other expectations hit. Knockout counts rose by at most one and no knockout seed reached the bar; dose
s2 rose by one and kept its two families; full s4 did not change. The cut-offs have not hidden successes that matter
for any verdict.

\paragraph{C2: the dose arm on an equal footing.} A second plain evaluation (sample seed 1) of dose r16 seeds 0, 1 and 2
proves 56, 39 and 30 strict theorems (first evaluation: 58, 39, 30) and acquires the same families on 3/3 seeds. The
guided evaluation proves 58, 42 and 29, again with the same families.

\paragraph{C3: textbook72 at the knockout's evaluation settings.} The old full bases s0, s1 and s2, evaluated at batch 512,
prove 34, 30 and 34 of textbook72's 72 problems, against 32, 32 and 38 in their original batch-2,048 evaluation. All
three are within the pre-registered $\pm4$, seed 2 at its edge. With all six full bases read at the knockout's settings,
the full-minus-knockout gap at r0 is 5.8 problems. The pre-registration wrote the range as $[-9, -4]$ for full minus
knockout, a sign slip; read as knockout minus full ($-5.8$), the gap is inside it.

\paragraph{C4: knockout seed 1's members at $n \geq K$.} Knockout seed 1's base drew 18,432 attempts on each of its 18
strict converse-contraposition members and on \texttt{la\_transfer\_916}, with no early stopping and new sampling seeds.
Knockout seed 3's base drew 18,432 on \texttt{916}. At $K = \Ktrain(16) = 17{,}189$, ``within reach'' ($\hat p \geq 1/K$)
needs at least two successes. Seed 1's base proved \texttt{916} 13 times, \texttt{1460} 4, \texttt{1096} and
\texttt{1606} 2 each, \texttt{55} and \texttt{1963} once each, and the other 13 members never, among them
\texttt{1246}, \texttt{2256}, \texttt{1019} and \texttt{528}. Seed 3's base proved \texttt{916} once. Two of the four
pre-registered expectations hit (\texttt{1460} within reach; \texttt{1019} at most one success). Two missed: at least
two of \texttt{1246}, \texttt{1460} and \texttt{2256} within reach (one was), and at least two successes on \texttt{916}
on both bases (seed 3's base had one; at its own $\Ktrain(16) = 20{,}782$ that is $\hat p \geq 1/K$, but the
pre-registration required two). By the pre-registered rule the paper drops ``mostly members its base could already reach''
(\S\ref{sec:ko-s1}). Pooled over the 18 members, the rate replicates the early-stopped sample's ($3.0$ against
$3.2\times10^{-5}$); the members that succeed do not.

\paragraph{C5: the pre-filter, in full.} Every text the pre-filter rejected in knockout seed 1's ladder (the gate dump,
rounds 1--16: 222,135 texts over 13 reasons, 22,522 of them on a by-contradiction step) was checked by Lean~4.34.1 with
the review's checker. The checker writes the statement header before the literal text, and accepts a text only if Lean
reports no error and the axioms are within \{\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}\}. None
was accepted. The one-sided 95\% bound on the pre-filter's false-reject rate in this ladder is $1.3\times10^{-5}$ overall
and $1.3\times10^{-4}$ on by-contradiction steps, as pre-registered. In the same dump, Lean decided every other text
(2,132,659 accepted, none rejected). A harness control, added here and not pre-registered, ran on the same pod with the
same code. It accepted all 400 of a random sample of texts the run's Lean gate had accepted, and none of the same 400
when each was paired with another sampled row's theorem.
