\section{Glossary}
\label{app:glossary}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.2em,style=nextline]
\item[ladder; r$N$; r0] one 16-round expert-iteration run from one pretrained seed; the model after $N$ rounds; the
pretrained base.
\item[evaluation; x0, x1; guided] $k=256$ independent attempts per theorem at $T=0.8$; two sample seeds; the same with
logically rejected steps redrawn (at most 10 per attempt).
\item[\sig, \sigp, lemma route, free positive reductio, classical-only, strict] see Table~\ref{tab:concepts}.
\item[acquired] at least 50\% of a family's strict held-out members proved in one evaluation.
\item[within reach / not reached / certified at $K$] $\hat p \geq 1/K$; 0 successes in $\geq K$ attempts; one-sided
95\% Clopper--Pearson bound $< 0.05/K$ (\S\ref{sec:setup}).
\item[\dcov] theorems RL proves at $k=256$ minus theorems the base reaches within $K$.
\item[\KT, \KTko, \KTdose] the full, knockout and 1\% dose pretraining sets (155,000 records each).
\end{description}

\section{Construction of the knockout set}
\label{app:k12ko}
\KTko{} removes every \KT{} record whose written or pruned proof applies \texttt{DN} to a derived double negation:
16,961 records, of which 16,703 are \sig{} and 258 cite an $\to$-elimination, $\lor$-elimination, $\bot$-elimination,
\texttt{DN} or $\land$-elimination line. It refills 16,961 records drawn uniformly, length by length, from \KT{}'s own
unused source pools: lengths up to 6 from the depth-3 generator pool and 7--12 from the long-proof pool. Every refill
record passes every \KT{} filter (no box depth $\geq 3$), applies \texttt{DN} only to assumptions or premises, and
contains no \sigp{} and no lemma route. Under an order-sensitive and a premise-order-invariant renaming key, the refill shares no
class with \KT{} or with any of the 100 data and target files of the repository. One of \KT{}'s 11 exclusion pools was
absent when the set was built and could not be checked. Every refill proof, translated to Lean, is accepted
(16,961/16,961). Unchanged proofs pass the same harness and goal-negated proofs fail it. Per-length counts are identical
to \KT{}'s. The dose set \KTdose{} swaps 1,550 refill records for removed \sig{} records at \KT{}'s \sig{} length mix.
A 0.1\% set is built but unused.

\section{Budgets at r16, the number of evaluation theorems, and the estimator}
\label{app:budgets}
At r16 the five allocations for full seeds s0/s1/s2 are $\Katt = 7{,}147$, $\Ktrain = 17{,}531/15{,}884/20{,}422$,
$\Kuni = 24{,}619/22{,}827/29{,}510$, $\Kpub = 47{,}962/49{,}428/50{,}447$ and
$\Kadapt = 109{,}875/100{,}929/119{,}575$ base attempts per theorem. $\Kadapt$ exceeds the 66,304 attempts most hard
theorems received, so the r16 sets at $\Kadapt$ are mostly undetermined.

Every $K$ scales as $1/N_{\mathrm{eval}}$. For the knockout certificates (Table~\ref{tab:certs}), spreading each
ladder's training-phase compute over the 106 strict theorems instead of the 322 evaluation theorems would triple $K$
(seed 0: 70,846) and void seed 0's certificate. Spreading it over 1,054 theorems would cut $K$ to 5,251--7,125;
then every base but seed 1 certifies (5 of 6). The certified count is thus 0, 1 or 5 of 6 at $N_{\mathrm{eval}} = 106$,
322 or 1,054.

$\hat p$, they condition on at least one success, which inflates $\hat p$ in the lowest bins.

\paragraph{Other measurements on the full seeds.} At matched gains in item-response-theory ability, EI flags no more
items as differentially improved than replay-only training. Eight RL rounds move the weights about as far as

\section{Two more knockout-base proofs in Lean}
\label{app:lean}
Both are large-$k$ successes of knockout bases before any RL. Lean~4.35.0 accepts each and rejects it with the goal
negated (\texttt{paper/scripts/lean\_examples.py}); the experiment's loop used Lean~4.34.1. (a) Knockout seed 1's base on \texttt{la\_transfer\_1246}, a held-out
converse-contraposition member: \sig{}, then \texttt{DN} in both branches of an $\lor$-elimination.
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem t (P Q R S : Prop) (h1 : (((*$\neg$*)S) (*$\to$*) ((*$\neg$*)((S (*$\land$*) R) (*$\lor$*) R)))) : (((S (*$\land$*) R) (*$\lor$*) R) (*$\to$*) S) := by
  have n1 : (((*$\neg$*)S) (*$\to$*) ((*$\neg$*)((S (*$\land$*) R) (*$\lor$*) R))) := h1
  have n12 : (((S (*$\land$*) R) (*$\lor$*) R) (*$\to$*) S) := (fun (n2 : ((S (*$\land$*) R) (*$\lor$*) R)) => by
    have n6 : ((*$\neg$*)((*$\neg$*)S)) := (fun (n3 : ((*$\neg$*)S)) => by
      have n4 : ((*$\neg$*)((S (*$\land$*) R) (*$\lor$*) R)) := n1 n3
      have n5 : False := n4 n2
      exact (n5 : False))
    have n11 : S := Or.elim n2 (fun (n7 : (S (*$\land$*) R)) => by
      have n8 : S := Classical.byContradiction (fun hh => n6 hh)
      exact n8) (fun (n9 : R) => by
      have n10 : S := Classical.byContradiction (fun hh => n6 hh)

\section{Power sampling}
\label{app:power}
The sampler targets $p^\alpha$ over whole proofs by Metropolis--Hastings with suffix resampling: 32 chains per theorem,
blocks of 4 proof steps, 8 MH steps per block (32 in a convergence check), $\alpha \in \{4, 2\}$ (and 1.5 in the
control). Acceptance rates were 0.55--0.74. On a development pilot the per-chain success was 0.752 at 8 MH steps per
block and 0.760 at 32. The plain comparators received exactly the decoded tokens of the power runs and took 1.6--2.7
times less wall-clock. Those of full s0 and s1 and knockout s0 kept no proof text, so their 1--2 successes could not be re-checked in Lean.
The others stored their accepted proofs (knockout s1's one, and the 8 and 15 distinct proofs behind the positive
control's 6/17 and 13/17): Lean~4.34.1 accepts all 24 and rejects all 24 with the goal negated (\emph{this paper}).

\section{Pre-registered expectations against outcomes}
\label{app:expectations}
The experiment's pre-registration stated numeric expectations. Tallied independently, they \emph{hit} on:
\begin{itemize}
\item the shape of \KTko{};
\item the general-ability gap on held-out greedy decoding and on non-\sig{} theorems;
\item 0/123 strict training theorems in round 1 on all knockout seeds, and 0/106 held-out strict theorems at r0 in the
x0 evaluation (the x1 evaluation found 1 on seed 1, and the guided evaluation 1, 1, 0, 1, 3, 0 across seeds 0--5);
\item knockout median log-probabilities at or below $-15$ nats;
\item converse contraposition acquired on 3/3 new full seeds;
\item at least one family on 6/6 full and 0/6 knockout seeds, the primary $p$ and the arm medians;
\item recovery of non-classical holdout250 by r16;
\item $\dcov > 0$ on 3/3 new full seeds;
\item the dose arm's round-1 \sig{} proofs and its families;
\item knockout seed 1's extension.
\end{itemize}
They \emph{missed} on:
\begin{itemize}
\item the classical-only share falling by $\geq 70\%$ (observed 67.6\%);
\item textbook72 within $\pm4$ at r0 (observed $-6.5$ over all 72 theorems with six full seeds, three read at another
batch; $-4.0$ on its 53 intuitionistic theorems with three);
\item certificates on $\geq 5/6$ knockout bases (observed 2/6 on the 105 family members, 1/6 on the pre-registered
106);
\item negated conditional on $\geq 2/3$ new full seeds (observed 1/3);
\item the first \sigp{} proof after round 4 (it came in round 1, on theorems supplying $\neg\neg G$);
\item $\dcov$ within $+8$ to $+35$ (two seeds at $+36$ and $+37$);
\item 3--15 strict training theorems in the dose arm's round 1 (observed 2/17/2);
\item a seeded-round gain on $\geq 3$ seeds (observed 1/5);
\item ``0/106 on the knockout bases for every sampler'' (plain sampling at $T=1$ solved \texttt{la\_transfer\_916} on
both bases).
\end{itemize}
Near-misses: the earlier full seed s1's base cost 8.04\,ms per attempt on an A40 (expected 6--8\,ms), the earlier full
seed s2's uniform-allocation budget at r8 was 0.61 times the published one (expected 0.4--0.6), and the dose arm's r16
count lay between the two arms' medians on 2/3 seeds. The
power-sampling expectations (0 solves) were met, but the sampler failed its positive control, so they are uninformative
rather than hits. Deviations from the plan: more than 0.1\% of some stratum was cut off in 64 of 74 plain evaluations;
the dose arm had no compute-matched reach measurement, no r16 redraw and no guided evaluation (the last two are added
here); and the refill's Lean check ran on the VPS rather than the first pod.

\section{Corrections applied}
\label{app:corrections}
Three Boschloo $p$-values in the experiment's reports (the exploratory RL-only test and two of the pre-registered
per-family tests) came from a transposed table. With the arms as
columns, the RL-only test is $p = 0.0032$ (reported 0.0020), excluded middle $0.39$ (0.30) and the three-versus-none
family tests $0.034$ (0.035). The primary $p = 0.00024$ and the dose test $p = 0.0033$ are unchanged.
\texttt{paper/scripts/stats\_extra.py} recomputes all of them. This paper also reports the knockout certificates on the
pre-registered 106-theorem pool: the experiment's reports and their review used the 105 family members, which certifies
seed 3 as well (Table~\ref{tab:certs}). The review's ten rewordings are applied throughout. The most important replace
``need'' with ``not acquired by this EI within 16 rounds'', use ``reach'' only in its technical sense, and state the
sharpening null for one sampler.
