- [Round 2, run 5: pattern dials on pools that require the pattern](2026-09-17-round2-run5-required-pools/README.md): completed and reviewed 2026-09-17; reductio at f = 0 is elicitation gated by the base rate and stalled at the 7-line stratum in this run; strict derived-ORE (cap 8) is amplification, ~10× block C once required.
- [Round 2, run 3: minimum outside data for ignition](2026-09-18-round2-run3-minimum-outside-data/README.md): completed and reviewed 2026-09-18; one sibling proof ignites every dead arm, four short generator instances ignite 3 / 4, the other pattern 0 / 4; the invalid-string control tested verifier acceptance, not structure, and is reworded.
- [Round 2, run 4: GRPO vs expert iteration at f = 0](2026-09-18-round2-run4-grpo-vs-ei/README.md): completed and reviewed 2026-09-18; at an equal sample budget on the same six Stage-1 draws, on-policy GRPO reaches the depth-3 pattern within the first 62 updates on every draw and ends 0.40–0.51 against expert iteration's 0.34–0.36, at the cost of held-out greedy 0.34–0.67; the draws are rare-prior, not zero-prior, so this is faster elicitation, not ignition from zero.
- [Round 2, run 2: six new patterns, classes pre-registered](2026-09-18-round2-run2-six-new-patterns/README.md): completed and reviewed 2026-09-18; at cap 6 none of six pre-registered patterns was acquired from a zero-base-rate draw (0 pattern theorems in 11 of 12 arms, nine of them with no valid proof of any target), the one mover is the one draw with a non-zero pre-RL rate (NEGI-with-ANDE s1, 0.005 → 0.147); at cap 8, four draws trained on no depth-4 proof already write one at 11–20 % per sample and EI takes them to 0.96–0.97, so the class labels predicted nothing the base rate did not.
- [Round 2, run 1: natural deduction in Lean, and novelty by model scale](2026-09-18-round2-run1-nd-to-lean/README.md): completed and reviewed 2026-09-18; the Lean translator agrees with `nd_verify` on 253,470 valid proofs and rejects all 4,012 mutations (2,906 of them by its Python structural pre-checks, 1,106 by Lean); Qwen3-Coder-30B in context proves 56 % of 236 theorems greedily in Lean against 26 % in the token format and 29 % in plain English (paired +0.31 [+0.26, +0.35], every draw, every bin), with the caveat that the Lean judge accepts any well-typed term while the ND judges demand one line format; the smallest Qwen3 that proves the RL-found theorems in Lean is 4B–14B for 90 % of them, median 8B in all three classes; the "opposite of the prediction" length claim is not supported (by box depth the gain is +0.35 / +0.22 / +0.35).
- [Round 3, run 2: the 8-line reductio stratum and the derived-ORE base-rate dependence](2026-09-18-round3-run2-eight-line-stratum/README.md): completed and reviewed 2026-09-18; every number reproduces; on the required reductio pool plus 45 six-liners, expert iteration ignited the 8-line stratum in 1 of 6 pre-registered sixteen-round arms and 0 of 3 k = 64 arms (2 of 8 sixteen-round arms overall, Wilson 95 % 0.07–0.59; same checkpoint, two sampling seeds: 54 vs 1), always a whole schema at a time; run 5's "zero-rate" f = 0 draws have six-line base rates (13 / 45 and 26 / 45 at pass@10⁴) and enter through them, one going on to 27 eight- and 25 nine-line targets, so run 5's 7-line wall was not a pattern gate; "needs rounds, not samples", "f is irrelevant" and derived-ORE "amplification proportional to base reach" (cap 8; s4 at 2.15 ×, Spearman 0.27 within f = 0) are not supported.
- [Round 3, run 1: pool composition or pattern class?](2026-09-18-round3-run1-pool-composition/README.md): completed and reviewed 2026-09-18; every number reproduces; on 16 new cap-6 Stage-1 draws (8 depth-3, 8 reductio), expert iteration ignites on the required-only pool in 1 of 16 and on required + 300 neighbours in 9 of 16 (Fisher p = 0.006); zero-rate draws 0 / 11 vs 4 / 11 (depth-3 3 of 6, reductio 1 of 5), and no zero-rate `req` arm ever takes a training step; the depth-3 neighbours are pattern-optional, not pattern-free: in all three zero-rate igniters the first depth-3 proof is on a neighbour a round before the first required one, and one of them (s26) writes depth-3 proofs on neighbours before any training; the reductio igniters acquire only the two schemata that are a neighbour proof plus one `DN` line (52–53 of 300), and one zero-rate draw reaches 151 hits / 300k on the required pool under neighbours-only training that never rewarded the pattern; "pattern-free neighbours" and "the asymmetry was the pools" are reworded, and the arm that would settle the depth-3 mechanism (exclusion on the neighbour stratum only) was not run.
- [Round 3, run 3: base generalisation by pattern class](2026-09-18-round3-run3-base-generalisation/README.md): completed and reviewed 2026-09-18; every number reproduces; of 24 new cap-6 f = 0 Stage-1 draws per set and with no RL, 15 emit a depth-3 proof (95 % 0.41–0.81; 2.0M samples per draw) and 12 a strict reductio proof (0.29–0.71; 600k), so the pre-registered separation fails and "structural patterns are generalised more readily by the base" is dropped; about a third of the zeros are detection-floor zeros (19 / 24 and 17 / 24 with ≈ 1M more samples on easy targets, rates repeat within 0.83–1.12); the reviewer's main finding, absent from the write-up, is a one-line length horizon — all 12,219 pattern hits in both classes are 7-line proofs (cap + 1), no draw writes an 8-line pattern proof, and on targets whose 7-line proof needs the pattern the two classes are tied at 12 / 24; strict derived-ORE's 0 / 24 rests on three required 7-line targets and supports no class ranking ("the pattern, not its class, sets the base rate" is reworded); no registered draw-level predictor works (first-half attempt rates ρ 0.04 / 0.14), and the `NEGI`-line loss correlation (−0.74, −0.42 on independent samples) is post hoc.
