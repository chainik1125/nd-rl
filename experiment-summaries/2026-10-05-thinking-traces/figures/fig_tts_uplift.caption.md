**Thinking traces against the no-think baseline at matched inference compute, 13–20-line proofs.** Top: pass@k against sampled tokens per problem on 986 problems. Sampled tokens are think + `</think>` + action + rejected redraws, with prompt prefill excluded. Each arm is read at k = tokens / (its mean sampled tokens per attempt), with linear interpolation in log k. The thinking-trace model (Stage 1 on honest-search thinking traces, then EI; solid) is read at a 64-token think budget per step with k = 32. The no-think baseline (Stage 1 on the same proofs without think blocks, then the same EI rounds; dashed) is read with k = 64. Columns are after EI round 1 (rl_hard12), round 2 (hard712 sample a; seeds 0 and 1) and round 3 (hard712 sample b). Each curve starts at one attempt (marker: pass@1) and ends at its last sampled attempt. In (b) and (c) the thinking model is also read with thinking off (budget 0, dotted, k = 64). The grey stroke marks the better of its two budgets at each token count. Bottom: thinking − baseline in percentage points, drawn only where both reads cover the token count. The grey segments are the best-budget difference where budget 0 is the better budget; elsewhere it equals the blue line. Bands are 95% bootstrap intervals over problems (4,000 draws shared by all arms; one training seed per arm, so seed variance is not included). Thinking is ahead per attempt (pass@1 +1.3 to +6.8 points) but costs 2.65–3.05× the tokens per attempt. At matched tokens it is behind at 1k and 2k in all four pairs. At 16k it is behind for round 3 (−7.1 [−10.2, −3.9]), tied for round 1 and round 2 seed 0, and ahead only for round 2 seed 1 (+7.6 [+4.1, +11.2]). For round 2 seed 0 the thinking model does best with thinking off at every token count, and then at best ties the baseline (−1.1 [−3.6, +2.2] at 16k).
