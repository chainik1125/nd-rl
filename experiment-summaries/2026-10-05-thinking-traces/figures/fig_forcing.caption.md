**Forcing a longer think lowers accuracy.** pass@1 when `</think>` is banned until the given number of think tokens per step (think budget capped at 256), for the Stage-1 thinking model before RL and the RL'd one-budget model (GRPO iteration 78), on 300 hard12 test problems (k = 16) and on textbook72 (72 problems, k = 32); bars are 95% hierarchical-bootstrap intervals over theorems (4,000 draws). Every forced minimum lowers pass@1, and a longer one lowers it more: the RL'd model falls from 94.1% to 46.4% on hard12 and from 32.9% to 5.3% on textbook72 at 128 tokens, and the Stage-1 model from 44.2% to 2.3% on hard12 already at 64. The RL'd model degrades less but still degrades, so for these models more forced thinking is not more useful thinking.
