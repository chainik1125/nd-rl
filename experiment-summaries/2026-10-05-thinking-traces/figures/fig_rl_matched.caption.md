**GRPO against EI at matched sampled attempts.** pass@1 (a, c) and pass@5 (b, d) on the hard12 test set (1,000 problems, k = 16, in distribution) and the 986 13–20-line proofs (k = 8, out of distribution), after one EI round or 39 GRPO iterations (160k sampled attempts) and after two rounds or 78 iterations (320k); no-thinking arms are read without a think budget and thinking arms at their full budget of 256 tokens per step. Bold lines are seed 0 with 95% hierarchical-bootstrap intervals over theorems (4,000 draws; one run each, so seed variance is not included), and light lines are GRPO seed 1 without intervals. In distribution GRPO is far ahead of EI at pass@1 (no thinking 92.8% vs 82.7% at 320k attempts) but not at pass@5 (96.6% vs 97.7%). On 13–20 the methods are close without thinking (GRPO +2.9 points pass@1 at 320k), and with thinking EI's second round (11.4%) is ahead of seed-0 GRPO, which falls from 11.9% to 6.2%, while GRPO seed 1 reaches 10.1%.
